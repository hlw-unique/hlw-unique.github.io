go for Beijing Roast Duck

now it’s afternoon.and having a class.

today no any classes before the noon.so i slept lately last noght.

therefore comes a late wake-up this morning.

i waked and just scrolled my phone for like about 30 minutes, during which i was appealed by the Roast Duck.

it has been a long time since i ate it last time.so now the food is of magically attaractive shape.

i couldn’t help but order one, and tackle a no-delivery option, which now looks a silly decision.

when it’s about 10:30, the NO.387 bus finally came and i started the little trip(only 4 stops before the destination).

with excited feelings, i walked to the Roast Duck restaurant as fast as i can.

(it’s not a restaurant, actually, which i quickly would know)

shocked as i was, i still walked into the “store” with a width only allowing two people come in.

instead of desks and seats for consumers’s convenience, the very scene i saw was the store owner’s baby girl playing on the climbing mat.

it’s not for in-house dining.

not a full-filled stomach ,but a parcel of food and an empty stomach followed me out of the house.

(but the duck tasted nice still.)
